A little more climb, a lot more view.
Half a day up to a Niseko ridge.

Our Premium Hike – Half Day steps up from the boardwalks: a moderate climb to a mirror pond, an open ridge, or a gondola-assisted summit — always with a guide setting a steady pace.

Some uphill, but nothing technical. Choose from three routes to match the day's weather and your group's legs.

On the Mountain

earned views
Come hands-free: a light meal, highland snacks and drinks (coffee or tea) are carried and served by us. We even bring a 500 ml water per guest, and a 20 L pack to borrow if you need one — so bring only what you personally want.

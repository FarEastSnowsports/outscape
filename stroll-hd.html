The Idea

Gentle trails, big country.
Four unhurried hours in the heart of Niseko.

Our Nature Stroll – Half Day is the easiest way to step into Hokkaido's highland scenery. Boardwalks across marshland, mirror ponds, and the slow drama of Mt. Yotei on the horizon — with a guide who reads the landscape for you.

No experience needed. We carry the snacks, set the pace, and leave room to simply look. Choose from five short courses — or let your guide pick the one at its best on the day.

From fresh green and lingering snow in early summer, through deep green highlands, to golden larch and crimson maple by late autumn — the same trail, never the same day.

Start & finish times follow the light

Daylight shifts a lot through the season — long, bright mornings in June; shorter, golden afternoons by October. Your guide proposes the best start and finish times to catch the conditions at their finest, and tailors them around your schedule.
